M3M Perform — UI design, round 1

Year strip, Home, Performance Log

Three artefacts below: the year strip at all three sizes with its colour rules, then the two screens at 1440px. Everything is drawn from the M3M brand kit — Carlito, navy headings, green section labels, white cards with the green top rule, tabular figures on every number.

Assumptions I made
Today is 3 March 2026. February is the open cycle and locks Saturday 7 March. FY 2025–26 runs Apr'25 → Mar'26.
Score is a weighted achievement percentage out of the employee's KRA weights, not a 1–5 rating. Ratings only appear in Reviews.
Lead sees all eight nav items; Calibration, Reports and Admin are dimmed with an HR tag rather than hidden, so the IA stays constant across roles.
"Lower is better" KRAs (TAT, escalations) invert the achievement maths — target ÷ actual. Flagged in the table so leads aren't confused.
Achievement is uncapped. 130% shows as 130% and pulls the weighted score up. Tell me if it should cap at 120.
Entry route is a segmented control at the top of Performance Log, so the spreadsheet path drops in without restructuring the screen.
